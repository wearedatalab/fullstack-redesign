FAQ

Frequently asked questions about FullStack

Short answers to what US teams ask before choosing an AI engineering partner.

What does FullStack do?

FullStack is an AI deployment and software engineering partner for US companies. FullStack Labs builds custom AI, agentic systems and software end to end, and FullStack Talent embeds vetted nearshore engineers in client teams. Founded in 2013, FullStack works with 600 US companies.

What is nearshore software development?

Nearshore software development means working with engineers in nearby countries that share your time zone. For US companies that usually means Latin America. The team works your business hours, so code reviews, standups and decisions happen the same day instead of overnight.

What is the difference between nearshore and offshore software development?

Offshore teams, for example in India or Southeast Asia, often have lower hourly rates but little overlap with US working hours. Nearshore teams in Latin America cost more per hour but work your hours. FullStack’s 2026 price guide lists roughly $40–$100 per hour for Latin America agencies and $25–$75 for India agencies.

How fast can FullStack start?

Vetted engineers can join your team in as little as 48 hours. For custom AI, a working proof of concept takes 6–8 weeks, and FullStack’s goal is frontier AI running in production in under 12 weeks.

How are AI agents deployed in production?

An AI agent reaches production when it is connected to real systems and data, tested against the workflow it will run and monitored after launch. That means defining the task and guardrails, integrating tools and permissions, measuring accuracy on real cases, keeping people in the loop for exceptions and tracking cost once live. Read the production checklist.

What are staff augmentation services?

Staff augmentation adds external engineers to your team for as long as you need them. Your leads direct the work; the provider sources and vets the talent. FullStack Talent offers individual engineers, pods and forward deployed engineers from a network of 1,100+ experts, and you can review coding challenges and interviews before you hire.

Is FullStack a US company?

Yes. FullStack Labs, Inc. is a US company founded in 2013 that serves US businesses. Its teams combine US-based experts with nearshore engineers in Latin America who work US business hours.
