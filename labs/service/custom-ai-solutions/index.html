In short

What is custom AI development?

Custom AI development is designing, training and deploying AI systems around one company’s own data, workflows and rules, instead of adapting the business to an off-the-shelf tool. FullStack starts with a proof of concept on a limited, secure data set in 6–8 weeks, then scales the same system into production.

Why start with a proof of concept: as many as 80% of AI projects fail before launch. A PoC shows what works on your data, validates security early and gives you a clear roadmap before you commit to a full build.

It depends on scope, data readiness and team seniority, so the most useful first number is the hourly rate of the team that builds it. Latin America agencies charge roughly $40–$100 per hour; US mid-market firms, $100–$300. A 6–8 week proof of concept is the lowest-risk way to size the full build.

FAQ

Custom AI development: frequently asked questions

Straight answers on timelines, cost, security and what happens after launch.

How long does it take to build a custom AI solution?

A working proof of concept takes 6–8 weeks. Moving from proof of concept to production depends on integrations, data and compliance; FullStack’s goal is frontier AI running in production in under 12 weeks. Vetted AI engineers can start in as little as 48 hours.

How much does an AI development company charge?

It depends on the type of firm and where the team is. FullStack’s 2026 price guide lists roughly $40–$100 per hour for Latin America agencies, $100–$300 for US mid-market firms and $250–$500+ for enterprise consultancies. Total cost depends on scope, so most projects start with a proof of concept to size the build.

What is an AI proof of concept (PoC)?

An AI proof of concept is a small working version of an AI system, trained on a limited data set, that shows whether the idea works before a full build. It reduces risk (as many as 80% of AI projects fail before launch), tests security in a safe environment and gives you a roadmap to production. See the PoC playbook.

What types of custom AI can FullStack build?

Agentic AI systems and AI agents, machine learning models for prediction and forecasting, natural language processing such as chatbots and document workflows, and computer vision for quality control and object detection. In production today: an AI call auditor, AI document processing and an agentic research assistant.

How is agentic AI different from traditional AI?

Traditional AI predicts or classifies within one narrow task. Agentic AI pursues a goal: it plans steps, uses tools and data, checks results and acts, often with several agents working together. That suits multi-step workflows, with guardrails and human review for exceptions. Read the full comparison.

How do you keep our data secure during AI development?

Proofs of concept are trained only on a limited data set in a controlled environment, so security can be validated before implementation. The PoC also produces a clear roadmap for how the system will handle your data in production.

Do you build AI for enterprises or small businesses?

Both. FullStack delivers AI for enterprise-scale organizations that need to optimize workflows and integrate with existing systems, and scalable AI solutions for small and mid-sized businesses. 600 US companies work with FullStack.

Can FullStack run the AI after launch?

Yes. The team that builds the proof of concept can take it to production and keep running and improving it, or transition it to your engineers.
